Guardrails

The rules that prevent expensive mistakes

  • Treehouse owns worktrees. Herdr opens them; harnesses do not create their own.
  • Unlanded work is preserved. A failed cleanup is investigated, never bypassed with force.
  • Unattended work is bounded. GNHF always has an iteration or token cap and no automatic push.
  • GUI evidence stays hidden. Rendering is judged analytically; tests never take the screen.
  • Delivery and merge authority are separate. Green checks do not invent permission to merge.
  • Releases stop at the local tag. Publishing remains a deliberate human action.
