## Literature verification

Check whether cited sources support the manuscript's claims. Use the active harness's literature
search/MCP and web capabilities; exact tool names are adapter details. The librarian owns PDF
acquisition and library summaries; consume or request those outputs rather than maintaining a second
library. A missing MCP service can fall back to primary-source web access.

### Inventory and bibliography

- Build a citation-to-source and source-to-location map once using the resolved spec/document and
  bibliography. Use docx-tools parsing where applicable; derive rendered numbering from the actual
  citation style and output, not raw BibTeX order.
- Check unresolved citations, duplicates, uncited entries, ordering requirements and inconsistent
  metadata. Handle main and supplement numbering according to their documented convention.
- Verify identifiers against authoritative publisher/repository records. A source may legitimately
  lack a DOI; absence alone is not evidence of an invalid reference.
- Check corrections, retractions and preprint/publication relationships with dated primary evidence.
  Lack of indexing or a venue score alone does not establish that a venue is predatory.
- Consult method/software authors' citation guidance for the version used.

### Source acquisition

For each load-bearing or quantitative claim: read a local full-text PDF if present; use existing
literature notes for triage; resolve metadata and open-access copies through available scholarly tools;
then consult the publisher or primary repository. Confirm critical numbers against source text.
If evidence remains unavailable, record UNVERIFIABLE with what was tried. Never manufacture a quote,
number, bibliographic fact or source-specific conclusion.

### Fidelity and framing

- Match number, denominator, metric, units, direction and scope to the actual source passage.
- Confirm attribution to the work that established the claim; distinguish primary experiments,
  reviews, hypotheses, modeled results and measured effects.
- Check method citations support the specific justification, not just the method's name.
- Preserve source caveats; a speculative statement cannot become an established fact when cited.
- Review figure/caption citations as well as body prose. Identify uncited load-bearing claims and
  propose verified references or narrower wording.
- Check novelty, dataset lineage and prior-work relationships from the target project's records and
  sources. No author, study or dataset has a preassigned framing in this shared skill.
- Check whether a newer source changes a claim presented as current, while retaining original
  historical/method references when appropriate.

### Verification ledger

One row per checked claim, including supported claims:

| location/anchor | claim as stated | citation | source consulted | verdict | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use SUPPORTED, PARTIAL, MISREPRESENTED or UNVERIFIABLE. Include the exact source location and the
correction or uncertainty. Append bibliography defects and missing-citation candidates with verified
identifiers. Convert defensible defects and unresolved load-bearing claims into findings, de-duplicate
with the other passes, then hand verified anchors to the emission procedure.
